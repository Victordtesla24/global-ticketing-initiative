<h2 style="color:#C9A84C;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:0.12em">VISION STATEMENT</h2>

Ticketalay is an Australian Marathi-diaspora cultural-events ticketing platform pursuing a staged expansion across Australia, the United Kingdom, the United States of America, Canada and the European Union. This repository holds the complete interactive proposal web application, which presents the platform's data intelligence architecture, market analysis and staged investment programme. It is designed to be read as a single, evidence-led narrative — from vision through architecture to a disciplined, gated commitment of capital.

<h2 style="color:#C9A84C;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:0.12em">DESIGN SYSTEM</h2>

The application is presented in the **AB Entertainment Design System**. The visual identity pairs a deep black canvas (`#0A0A0A`) with a signature gold primary (`#C9A84C`) and a muted foreground (`#A0A0A0`). Typography uses two custom families — **AB Marquee** for display and headings, and **AB Sans** for body text. The interface is built from glass-card components, ornament dividers (`◆ ─────`) and uppercase, wide-tracked headings that carry gold accents.

Font files (AB&nbsp;Marquee and AB&nbsp;Sans, in Light through Black weights) reside in [`public/fonts/`](public/fonts/), and the design-token reference — colour, radius, typography and motion variables — is wired through [`tailwind.config.ts`](tailwind.config.ts).

<h2 style="color:#C9A84C;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:0.12em">DATA ARCHITECTURE</h2>

The platform is founded on a governed medallion lakehouse on Amazon Web Services, progressing data through Bronze, Silver and Gold layers. Curated Gold data feeds five purpose-built data marts — Finance &amp; Unit Economics, Customer &amp; Consent, Events / Venues / Inventory, Marketing &amp; Growth, and Markets / Partners / Risk. An activation layer then serves this intelligence to campaign targeting, business-intelligence dashboards and machine-learning model feeds, with consent, data quality, lineage and residency controls applied throughout. All figures are expressed in Australian dollars and are estimated.

<h2 style="color:#C9A84C;text-transform:uppercase;letter-spacing:0.12em">INVESTMENT PROGRAMME</h2>

The proposal advances a staged, evidence-led investment programme in which expenditure follows evidence through gated release. It is structured as three sequential, gated phases — Foundation, Discovery and Pilot — each with a capped budget and a defined decision gate: Foundation at AUD 35K–80K, Discovery at AUD 90K–180K, and Pilot at AUD 300K–500K. The foundation work leads the sequence, establishing the commercial and legal basis before further capital is committed. The full three-year total cost of ownership for the base programme is AUD 12.62m [EST].
